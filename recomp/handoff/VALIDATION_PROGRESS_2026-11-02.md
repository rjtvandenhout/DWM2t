# Additional Cobi validation — 2 November 2026

These are additional checks against the previously verified Cobi/CGB build. They do not establish every postgame path, random seed, link transaction, Tara content or SGB hardware mode. Original saves were retained separately; gameplay used independent genuine Journal copies and original controller input. Squiz was not used for breeding.

## Completed additional observations

- [FirstField](evidence/2026-11-02-firstfield/README.md): a second authentic random key was won in the original C Class and appraised. Its world (seed `0x1927`) was generated, saved and freshly continued. The exact released production EXE separately generated the world and reloaded its original Journal under Wine; the full 32 KiB save remained byte-identical after Continue. This supplements the previously observed QuietSea (`0x16c0`) world, not an exhaustive seed test.
- [Two different key trade](evidence/2026-11-02-link-distinct-key-trade/README.md): two original Cobi players exchanged genuine QuietSea and FirstField keys under Wine using the exact released production EXE. Both real Journals were separately continued, and each player's monster records, party and quest bytes were checked. The README explains the inherited generic VS fields in the raw fresh-Continue report; the separate Continue events and actual saves remain available as evidence.
- [Original fixed-key refusals](evidence/2026-11-02-link-key-refusal/README.md): Pirate, Oasis and GreatLog were refused for trading by the original game. Cancelling returned both players to the title and fresh Continue preserved both starting Journals byte-for-byte.
- [Arena B](evidence/2026-11-02-arena-b/README.md): an initial original-input attempt won round one and lost round two. A later genuine Journal funded by original BlackIslet dungeon rewards enabled the stronger ending party to pay 500G and win all three B-class matches. The original game awarded a Magic Key, and the champion Journal was freshly continued byte-for-byte. On that save the original A-class menu asked for 1000G and refused the remaining 1G. Items were prohibited during the earlier B attempt.
- [BlackIslet world search](evidence/2026-11-02-dream-egg-world-search/README.md): an original FirstField dungeon awarded another Magic Key. After appraisal, it generated BlackIslet (seed `0xc887`). Two original five-floor dungeons yielded VITbelt and MagicBelt; their Journal and fresh Continue were checked. The observed rewards did not include DreamEgg. This is specific gameplay under Wine, not all seeds.
- [B-class prize key trade](evidence/2026-11-02-arena-b-prize-key-trade/README.md): the original B-class prize key was exchanged for FirstField between two genuine Cobi saves, each followed by a fresh Continue. The receiving player appraised it as BlueHill (seed `0x23b7`), generated the world and saved/reloaded it; its observed rewards did not contain DreamEgg. This is one original exchange and one seed under Wine.
- [Further original worlds](evidence/2026-11-02-dream-egg-world-search/README.md): an A-class prize generated FirstMoon (`0xdc33`); an earlier genuine key generated [QuietCrag](evidence/2026-11-02-quietcrag/README.md) (`0xb7c5`), whose original dungeon awarded the FirstPond key (`0x5a59`). A FirstPond dungeon awarded the GreenMtn key (`0x427d`). Each named world was appraised, generated, saved and freshly continued using the original game. None of their observed reward arrays showed DreamEgg `$81`; this is not a proof for other seeds.
- [BlueHill and Arena A](evidence/2026-11-02-bluehill-arena-a/README.md): an original three-floor dungeon awarded a PltnmCape, which was saved, freshly continued and sold for 3750G. That separate save won B, then entered A for 1000G. Earlier tactics lost; a later original-input attempt won all three A matches using Defense/Charge/Charge. Garon used HealAll during one fight; the A-class prize Journal passed a byte-identical fresh Continue. S was offered for 5000G and refused with 2356G. These observations were made on the diagnostic build under Wine.
- [Arena S attempts](evidence/2026-11-02-arena-s/README.md): original BlueHill rewards and a QuietCrag dungeon item were sold through game menus, funding the 5000G entry on an authentic A-champion line. Three S attempts won the first battle but lost the second. Before the last attempt Garon was legitimately trained from level 40 to 41; during the second battle Derzo revived him, but the party still lost. The pre-S and trained Journals were separately saved and freshly continued; no S-class victory is claimed.
- [Additional S timing batch](evidence/2026-11-02-arena-s-batch/README.md) and [independent variants](evidence/2026-11-02-arena-s-variants/README.md): ten bounded original-input attempts began from the same genuine level-41 Journal, varying only normal controller choices and waits. Two reached the same reproduced third-fight state and both lost; they are two follow-up choices from the same deterministic preceding sequence, not two independent third-fight states. No S victory or prize was observed.
- [Bounded training investigation](evidence/2026-11-02-arena-training/README.md): starting from a genuine level-41 Journal, 17 further original-input battles were won with supervised recovery, Journal and byte-identical fresh Continue checkpoints. The final save preserves the original party and quest data. The controller stopped at unexpected recruitment messages, which were handled manually. Independent review found residual uncertainty in general menu recognition, so unattended training was not claimed safe and no subsequent S victory is claimed.
- [Further supervised training](evidence/2026-11-02-arena-training-continuation/README.md) added 20 original-input wins across seven distinct save/reload cycles; Derzo reached level 41, and the last Journal remained byte-identical after Continue. The [post-training S attempt](evidence/2026-11-02-arena-s-final20/README.md) reached the third fight and lost. Five [additional variants](evidence/2026-11-02-arena-s-trained-final-variants/README.md) also lost; two used different follow-up actions from the same reproduced third-fight state. None establishes an S championship.
- A [second supervised 20-win training series](evidence/2026-11-02-arena-training-second20/README.md) was recorded with seven further genuine Journal/Continue pairs. The [S attempt from its last checkpoint](evidence/2026-11-02-arena-s-final40/README.md) won rounds one and two and lost round three. The checkpoint remains intact with 5688G, SHA-256 `0a3f752db96121462cc5edae965a4b2c2f6d9d64e331d236896c53d288fb08ea`; in this workspace a copy is at `outputs/validation-2026-11-02/arena-s-latest.sav` (the Journal is not committed). Further gameplay and training are needed; none of these trials establishes an S prize or pro-class result.
- [FirstField active-key refusal and hub trade](evidence/2026-11-02-firstfield-hub-trade/README.md): the original game refused the current active world key, then accepted FirstField exchange after original return to GreatLog and Journal. Both players' fresh Continues preserved their checked state. A prior looted dungeon chest remained empty after the exchange; do not treat the cache transition as a repeatable reward.
- [Original-ROM comparison of BlueHill rewards](evidence/2026-11-02-bluehill-original-pyboy/README.md): after one original trainer victory, the diagnostic build under Wine awarded repeated meat items until the 24-slot inventory was full. Two independent runs of the original Cobi ROM and genuine pre-event Journal under PyBoy reproduced the same 16-item/full-inventory/close pattern. Exact rewards and input timings differed; this comparison does not establish every aspect of the event or prove behavior on physical hardware.
- The original ROM was independently rebuilt and matched SHA-256 `8e38a7420ef5239c7196aa4fd4b1499e65db2823e875bbf8241bb2199e766d4e`. Five Linux runtime-core tests passed without skips; their [report](evidence/2026-11-02-runtime-core/runtime-core-validation.json) and [CTest XML](evidence/2026-11-02-runtime-core/runtime-core-tests.xml) are preserved (the report's scratch paths and test binaries are not included).
