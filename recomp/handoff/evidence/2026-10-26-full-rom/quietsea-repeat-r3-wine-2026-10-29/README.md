The recorded inventory therefore establishes one SteelFang in this run, not two. The displayed wallet was 11G; the 500G Arena B entry and its victory remain unproven. This observation does not prove a different random world, DreamEgg acquisition, every key exchange or full-game acceptance. A Journal menu was used after the pickup, but this evidence does not claim a newly exported, independently reloaded reward save.
