# Five bounded S Class attempts from the level-41 Journal

Five independent continuations of the genuine trained Garon Journal, SHA-256 `a261d433e47f1ad62fbcdf43e6095d49afd170ad0ebe2aedf22f4964b572f797`, were tested in the original game on the diagnostic full-ROM executable SHA-256 `61a0e6d3ff84b5322b101247fc93ff6889f123a1bcd94df663135757fb3b9855` under Wine. Each starts from the same unchanged 5688G Journal in its own fresh runtime, with original P1 inputs only; the runtime selected the RNG consequences. No state, memory, save, items, or RNG were patched, and Squiz was not bred. The Journal's independent fresh Continue and training provenance are recorded in the [Arena S evidence](../2026-11-02-arena-s/README.md).

1. A 100-frame idle before selecting S, with Garon Defense / Slash Charge / Derzo Charge, [won battle one](attempt1-round1-win.bmp). [Battle two started](attempt1-round2-start.bmp) with Derzo alone at 173 HP; his Defense revival attempt failed, followed by [defeat](attempt1-loss.bmp).
2. A 200-frame idle and the same initial tactics [won battle one](attempt2-round1-win.bmp). [Battle two started](attempt2-round2-start.bmp) with Slash 62 HP, Derzo 132 HP and Garon unconscious; Slash Charge / Derzo Defense ended in [defeat](attempt2-loss.bmp).
3. A 300-frame idle and the same initial tactics led to Garon alone during battle one. The original player changed his tactic from Defense to Charge and [won battle one](attempt3-round1-win.bmp). [Battle two started](attempt3-round2-start.bmp) with only Garon at 35 HP; Defense was followed by [defeat](attempt3-loss.bmp).
4. A 400-frame idle and the same initial tactics [won battle one](attempt4-round1-win.bmp). [Battle two started](attempt4-round2-start.bmp) with all three alive: Garon 222, Slash 181, Derzo 98 HP. Repeating Defense / Charge / Charge still ended in [defeat](attempt4-loss.bmp).
5. A separately recorded original 147-frame route from the same Journal was reproduced through the [third battle's FIGHT menu](attempt5-round3-start.bmp), matching Slash 75, Derzo 207 and Garon 0 HP. Changing Slash to Charge and Derzo to Defense ended in [defeat](attempt5-loss.bmp). This was a variation of the separate 147-frame original route, not an additional outcome of the first four attempts.

Each attempt's complete original input commands and diagnostic trace are retained as `attemptN-p1-commands.txt` and `attemptN-p1-trace.txt`. Independent snapshots insert no game input or frames. The saved screenshots are original diagnostic captures; cartridge saves and ROM data are excluded. These tests establish five particular results, not that S Class is impossible. No S Class victory or post-victory Journal is claimed.
